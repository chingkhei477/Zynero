Questions

Good to know before you shop.

Short answers here. The full rules live in our Cashback Policy.

Where does ZYNERO cashback come from?

Retailers pay ZYNERO a commission when you shop through our link. We share part of that commission with you as cashback. The rate for each retailer is shown on its offer card.

When is my cashback confirmed?

Only after the retailer confirms your order. Most retailers wait until their return or cancellation window has closed, so confirmation can take several weeks. Each offer card shows the usual confirmation time for that retailer.

Why would cashback be declined?

The usual reasons are a cancelled, returned or refunded order, a coupon that wasn't allowed for the offer, or an order that didn't meet the offer's conditions. Every reason is covered in our Cashback Policy.

How do I withdraw?

Once your confirmed cashback reaches ₹100, you can request a payout to a UPI ID in your own name. UPI is the only payout method.

My cashback hasn't tracked. What now?

Wait until the offer's tracking time has passed, then tell us with your order ID, order date, amount and the retailer's confirmation email. We'll raise it with the retailer.
