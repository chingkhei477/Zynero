About ZYNERO

Cashback, minus the guesswork.

ZYNERO is a cashback and rewards platform for shoppers in India, based in Manipur.

Cashback should feel like a small, honest bonus — not a puzzle. So we built ZYNERO around one idea: tell people the rate, tell them every condition, and show them exactly where their money is.

How we work

Four principles, in writing.

Clarity first

Every offer shows its rate, conditions, tracking time and confirmation time on the card itself.

Only verified offers

An offer goes live only when tracking with the retailer works and its terms are confirmed.

Respect for your data

We collect only what we need to track and pay cashback and to reply to you — and we never sell it.

People who answer

Questions, missing cashback and payout issues go to a real inbox, read by real people.

How ZYNERO earns

Our income is your cashback's source.

Retailers pay ZYNERO a commission when a purchase made through our link is confirmed. We pass part of that commission to you as cashback. That's the whole model.

Because cashback comes from the retailer's commission, it's paid only after the retailer confirms your order.
