How it works

Cashback, from click to UPI.

Here is exactly what happens between tapping an offer and receiving money in your UPI app — including the parts that depend on the retailer.

  1. 01

    Choose an eligible offer

    Open Explore Offers and read the card. The rate and every condition are listed there.

    • Cashback applies only to purchases that start from a ZYNERO offer link.
    • The order must meet the offer's conditions, such as a minimum order value or excluded categories.
    • You need to be 18 or older, in India, with a UPI ID in your own name.
  2. 02

    Shop — and get tracked

    Tap “Shop via ZYNERO”. The retailer's site opens and records that your visit came from us. Pay as you normally would.

    • Complete the purchase in the same visit and browser.
    • Pause ad blockers and don't use coupons the offer doesn't allow.
    • The purchase usually shows as Tracked within the offer's tracking time.
  3. 03

    The retailer approves it

    Retailers confirm cashback only after an order can no longer be returned or cancelled. Until then it stays Pending.

    • Each offer card shows the retailer's usual confirmation time.
    • Returned, cancelled or refunded orders are Declined or reduced.
    • The decision belongs to the retailer; we follow up if something looks wrong.
  4. 04

    Withdraw to UPI

    When your Confirmed cashback adds up to ₹100 or more, request a payout to your UPI ID.

    • UPI is the only payout method.
    • The UPI ID must belong to you. We may verify it before paying.
    • Once sent, the cashback shows as Paid.

Missing cashback

Didn't track? We'll chase it.

Once the offer's tracking time has passed — and while the order is still inside the retailer's confirmation period — send us:

  • Retailer name, order ID, order date and amount
  • The retailer's order confirmation email or a screenshot of it
  • The email address you used
Report missing cashback
Do I need to do anything after buying?

No. Keep your order confirmation email and wait for the status to move. If nothing has tracked once the offer's tracking time has passed, contact us.

Can I get cashback on an order I've already placed?

No. Cashback only applies to purchases that start from a ZYNERO offer link, because that is how the retailer knows the order came through us.

What if I return part of my order?

Cashback is recalculated on the part you keep. Returned, cancelled or refunded items don't earn cashback.

Why UPI only?

UPI is instant, widely used across India and lets us send payouts directly to an ID in your name, with no card or bank details shared on our side.
