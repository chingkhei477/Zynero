Before you shop

Six habits that keep your cashback tracking.

Tracking depends on the retailer recognising that you came from ZYNERO. These steps give it the best chance.

  • Start from ZYNERO every time. Tap “Shop via ZYNERO” right before you buy, even if you have visited the store already.

  • Finish in one visit. Complete the payment in the same browser session you opened from ZYNERO.

  • Pause ad blockers. Some blockers and strict privacy modes stop the retailer from recording the visit.

  • Use only allowed coupons. Codes from other websites can cancel cashback. Check the offer's conditions.

  • Add items after clicking through. Items already in your cart before the visit may not track.

  • Keep your order email. You'll need the order ID and confirmation if you ever raise a missing-cashback query.
